# Stages of labor and delivery (parturition)
(Starter note. Verify against your hospital protocol.)

- First stage: regular contractions with cervical dilation. Latent phase to about 5 cm (WHO) or 6 cm (ACOG), then active phase with faster dilation. Typical contractions: every 3 to 5 minutes, lasting 60 seconds, for an hour.
- Second stage: full dilation (10 cm) to birth of the baby. Mother pushes. Monitor fetal heart rate regularly.
- Third stage: birth of the baby to delivery of the placenta, usually within 30 minutes. Active management (oxytocin 10 IU intramuscular after birth, controlled cord traction, uterine massage) reduces bleeding.
- Fourth stage: first 1 to 2 hours after birth. Check bleeding, uterine tone, blood pressure, pulse and the baby's breathing and temperature. Skin-to-skin contact and early breastfeeding.
- Go to the hospital when contractions are regular and strong, the waters break, bleeding occurs, or the baby moves less.
- Normal fetal heart rate is 110 to 160 beats per minute.
