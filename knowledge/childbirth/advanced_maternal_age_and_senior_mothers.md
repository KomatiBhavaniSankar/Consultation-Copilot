# Care for older mothers (35 years and above)
(Starter note. Verify against your local guideline.)

- Higher risk of gestational diabetes, high blood pressure and pre-eclampsia, placenta previa, preterm birth, stillbirth and caesarean birth. Chromosomal conditions become more likely.
- Offer early screening: blood pressure and urine checks every visit, early glucose testing, detailed anomaly scan, growth scans in the third trimester.
- Low-dose aspirin may be considered for those with risk factors for pre-eclampsia; decide with the obstetrician.
- Discuss timing and mode of birth. Many guidelines suggest talking about induction at about 39 weeks for women 35 and over, balancing risks.
- Review pre-existing conditions and medicines (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid) before and during pregnancy.
- Plan for birth in a unit that can manage emergencies. Monitor labor closely; senior staff should know the risk status.
