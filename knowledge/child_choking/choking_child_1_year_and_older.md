# Choking in a child 1 year or older
(Starter note. Replace or extend with your reviewed documents.)

Signs of severe choking: cannot speak, cough or breathe; clutching the throat; silent; lips turning blue.
A child who can cough forcefully, cry or speak should be encouraged to keep coughing.

## Steps
1. Have someone call the emergency number, or use speaker.
2. Back blows: lean the child forward, support the chest, give up to 5 firm blows between the shoulder blades.
3. Abdominal thrusts: stand or kneel behind the child. Make a fist just above the belly button and below the ribs, hold it with the other hand, give up to 5 sharp thrusts inward and upward.
4. Alternate 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts until the object comes out.
5. If the child becomes unresponsive: lay them on the floor, call emergency services and begin CPR. Before each set of breaths, look in the mouth and remove the object only if visible.
6. After any abdominal thrusts, the child must be checked at a hospital because of the risk of internal injury.

## Never
- No blind finger sweeps. Do not give water. Do not hang the child upside down.
