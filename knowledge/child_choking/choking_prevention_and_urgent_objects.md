# Choking prevention and swallowed objects
(Starter note.)

- High-risk foods for young children: whole grapes, nuts, popcorn, hard sweets, raw carrot pieces, hot dog rounds, chunks of meat or cheese. Cut food small and keep the child seated while eating.
- Small objects: coins, beads, marbles, balloons, button batteries and small magnets.
- Button batteries and multiple magnets are emergencies even if the child is breathing normally. Go to hospital at once. Do not let the child eat or drink, and do not induce vomiting.
- Coughing, drooling, refusing to eat, chest pain or wheeze after a choking episode all need a doctor's review.
