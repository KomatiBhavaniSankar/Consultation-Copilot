# Cardiac arrest: CPR and AED
(Starter note. Verify against your local resuscitation guideline.)

- Recognise: unresponsive and not breathing normally (or only gasping). Check breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
- Call emergency services and get an AED. Start compressions at once.
- Adult compressions: centre of the chest, 100 to 120 per minute, 5 to 6 cm deep, allow full recoil, minimise pauses. Trained rescuers give 2 breaths after 30 compressions.
- Child: one or two hands, about one third of chest depth. Infant: two fingers, about one third of depth.
- AED: switch on, attach pads, follow voice prompts. Do not touch the person when it analyses or shocks. Resume CPR straight after the shock.
- ALS: shockable rhythms (VF, pulseless VT) shock then 2 minutes CPR. Adrenaline 1 mg IV every 3 to 5 minutes. Amiodarone 300 mg after the third shock, then 150 mg.
- Treat reversible causes: hypoxia, hypovolaemia, hypo/hyperkalaemia, hypothermia, tension pneumothorax, tamponade, toxins, thrombosis.
- Rotate compressors every 2 minutes. Plan post-arrest care.
