# Diabetes: diagnosis and types
(Starter note based on ADA criteria. Follow your local guideline where it differs.)

## Diagnosis
- HbA1c 6.5% or more; prediabetes 5.7 to 6.4%.
- Fasting plasma glucose (8 hours no food) 126 mg/dL (7.0 mmol/L) or more; prediabetes 100 to 125 mg/dL.
- 2-hour glucose in a 75 g OGTT 200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) or more; prediabetes 140 to 199 mg/dL.
- Random glucose 200 mg/dL or more with classic symptoms (thirst, frequent urination, weight loss).
- Without clear symptoms, repeat the test to confirm.

## Types
- Type 2: adults, overweight, gradual onset, family history, inactivity. Most common.
- Type 1: autoimmune, often young, sudden onset, weight loss, ketones. Needs insulin.
- LADA: slow-onset autoimmune diabetes in adults. MODY: strong family history across generations, young onset.
- Gestational: first found in pregnancy; screen at 24 to 28 weeks with a 75 g OGTT.
- Secondary: steroids, pancreatic or endocrine disease.

## Warning signs needing same-day care
Fruity breath, rapid deep breathing, vomiting, abdominal pain, drowsiness or confusion may mean diabetic ketoacidosis.
