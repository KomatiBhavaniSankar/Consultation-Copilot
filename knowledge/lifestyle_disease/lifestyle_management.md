# Lifestyle management of diabetes and related conditions
(Starter note. Replace or extend with your reviewed documents.)

## Food
- Use the plate method: half non-starchy vegetables, a quarter lean protein, a quarter whole grains or other high-fibre carbohydrate.
- Cut sugary drinks, sweets and refined flour. Choose whole grains, pulses, nuts, vegetables and fruit in sensible portions.
- Regular meal times help glucose control, especially on insulin or sulfonylureas.

## Activity and weight
- At least 150 minutes a week of moderate activity (brisk walking), spread over most days, plus muscle-strengthening twice a week.
- A short walk after meals lowers glucose. Break up long sitting every 30 minutes.
- Losing 5 to 10% of body weight improves glucose, blood pressure and cholesterol.

## Monitoring and targets
- HbA1c target below 7% for many adults; individualise for age and other illness.
- Blood pressure target usually below 130/80. Check lipids yearly.
- Yearly checks: eyes (retina), kidneys (eGFR and urine albumin-creatinine ratio), feet.
- Daily foot check: look for cuts, blisters, redness; wear well-fitting shoes; never walk barefoot.

## Medicines
- Metformin is usually first-line for type 2 diabetes unless contraindicated. Add-ons depend on heart, kidney disease and weight.
- Low blood sugar (below 70 mg/dL): take 15 g fast sugar, recheck in 15 minutes, repeat if still low.

## Other habits
- Stop smoking, limit alcohol, sleep 7 to 8 hours, manage stress. Poor sleep and stress raise glucose.
- Sick days: keep drinking fluids, keep checking glucose, and call the doctor if you cannot keep food or fluids down.
